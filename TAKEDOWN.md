# Takedown requests

We only host public-domain, Creative Commons and contributor-owned music, and we remove
anything that does not meet that bar as soon as we learn of it.

To report content you believe infringes your copyright, open a GitHub issue titled
`Takedown: <title>` at https://github.com/chiElephant/guitar-tutor/issues with:

1. The URL or file path of the content.
2. The work you believe is infringed and your relationship to it.
3. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised.
4. Your name and a way to reach you.

We will remove or disable the content while we review, reply within 10 business days, and
remove repeat infringers' contributions. Contributors may respond with a counter-notice
showing the work is public domain or properly licensed.
