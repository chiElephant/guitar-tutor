This is the test, and you set it yourself. Record it in the app so you can hear what a listener hears. The point is honest listening.

## The task

Play **three songs** from this grade at **60 bpm** with the metronome or the library recording, **all the way through, with clean chord changes**. Clean means: every chord rings, the change lands on beat 1, and your strumming arm never stops.

Suggested set, one from each style:

1. **Soldier's Joy** (D, A): four down-strums per bar. [Play Soldier's Joy](piece:soldiers-joy)
2. **Amazing Grace** (G, C, D, Em): three down-strums per bar. [Play Amazing Grace](piece:amazing-grace)
3. **Auld Lang Syne** (G, D, Em, C): D DU UDU. [Play Auld Lang Syne](piece:auld-lang-syne)

Swap any of them for Old Joe Clark, Scarborough Fair, The Water Is Wide, Greensleeves or Shady Grove if you prefer.

## Listening back

Play your recording and ask, in this order: Is the beat steady? Do the chords ring? Are the changes on time? Fix the first "no" you hear, not all of them at once. A steady beat with a muffled chord is a small problem; a perfect chord that arrives late is a bigger one.

## When you are ready for Grade 2

If all three songs are clean at 60 bpm, you are ready. If two are, spend one more week on the third. Grade 2 adds the F chord, seventh chords, the twelve-bar blues, fingerpicking and your first scale.

## Practice

- Record the three songs. Listen back once without the guitar in your hands.
- Note the single weakest change and do one-minute changes on it.
- Re-record the weakest song tomorrow and compare.
