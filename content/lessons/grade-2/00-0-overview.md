Grade 2 assumes the eight open chords, D DU UDU and clean changes at 60 bpm from Grade 1. It adds the first barre, seventh chords and the blues, fingerpicking, two scales and reading rhythms. Lesson titles below are the plan; lessons are written one module at a time.

## Module 1: The F chord
- The mini-barre: F with two strings under one finger (chord-f, mini-barre)
- Changes to F from C and Dm (change-c-f, change-dm-f)
- Song: Shady Grove in D minor with F (song-shady-grove, chord-f)

## Module 2: Sus chords
- Asus2 and Asus4: colouring A (chord-asus2, chord-asus4)
- Dsus2, Dsus4 and Esus4 (chord-dsus2, chord-dsus4, chord-esus4)
- Sus riffs over D and A (sus-riff)

## Module 3: Seventh chords and the blues
- A7, D7 and E7 (chord-a7, chord-d7, chord-e7)
- The 12-bar blues in A (blues-12-bar, shuffle)
- G7 and B7 (chord-g7, chord-b7)

## Module 4: Palm muting
- Resting the hand: muted power chords (palm-mute, power-chords)
- A muted riff in E (palm-mute, riff-2)

## Module 5: Eighth-note and syncopated strumming
- All eight: D U D U D U D U with accents (strum-eighths)
- Missing the beat: syncopated patterns (strum-syncopated)
- Songs: Old Joe Clark and The Water Is Wide with new patterns (strum-syncopated, song-old-joe-clark, song-the-water-is-wide)

## Module 6: Fingerpicking
- p-i-m-a: naming the fingers, thumb on the bass (fingerpick-pima)
- Arpeggio patterns on Em, Am, C and G (fingerpick-arpeggio)
- Song: Scarborough Fair fingerpicked (fingerpick-arpeggio, song-scarborough-fair)

## Module 7: First scales
- The C major scale in open position (scale-c-major)
- Minor pentatonic box 1 in A (scale-minor-pentatonic-box-1)
- Melodies: The Ash Grove and Ode to Joy in full (melody-2, scale-c-major)

## Module 8: Reading rhythm
- Quarters, eighths and rests (rhythm-read)
- Dotted notes and ties (rhythm-read)
- Clapping tunes from the library before you play them (rhythm-read)

## Module 9: Four-chord songs and the capo
- Transposing with the capo: finding the key for your voice (capo-transpose)
- Songs: Amazing Grace in any key, Greensleeves with E7 (capo-transpose, song-amazing-grace, song-greensleeves)
- Grade 2 checkpoint: a blues, a fingerpicked song and a strummed song at 80 bpm (checkpoint-grade-2)
