Grade 3 turns the shapes you know into a map of the whole neck: movable barre chords, the CAGED system, two pentatonic boxes with the expressive techniques that make them sing, and the number system that lets you work out songs by ear. It finishes with classical studies and a consolidation checklist. Lesson titles below are the plan.

## Module 1: Barre chords
- The E-shape barre: F, G, A anywhere on the neck (barre-e-shape)
- The A-shape barre: Bb, B, C (barre-a-shape)
- Minor barres and a song: Scarborough Fair with barres (barre-e-shape-minor, barre-a-shape-minor, song-scarborough-fair)

## Module 2: CAGED
- Five shapes, one chord: C up the neck (caged)
- Finding any major chord from its root (caged, fretboard-notes)
- Practice loop: one progression in three positions (caged)

## Module 3: Pentatonic boxes and expression
- Minor pentatonic box 1 (scale-minor-pentatonic-box-1)
- Box 2 and joining it to box 1 (scale-minor-pentatonic-box-2)
- Hammer-ons, pull-offs and slides (hammer-on, pull-off, slide)
- Bends: half-step and whole-step in tune (bend)

## Module 4: Triads
- Major and minor triads on strings 1 to 3 (triads-top-3)
- Triads on strings 2 to 4 and inversions (triads-inversions)
- Song: The Water Is Wide with triads (triads-top-3, song-the-water-is-wide)

## Module 5: The number system
- I, IV, V and vi in any key (number-system)
- Working out a song by ear (ear-progressions, number-system)
- Transposing without a capo (number-system, barre-e-shape)

## Module 6: Playing with a band
- Playing to a backing track: locking to drums (backing-track)
- Rhythm and lead: switching roles over a 12-bar (backing-track, blues-12-bar)
- Jamming on a two-chord vamp with box 1 and 2 (improvise-pentatonic)

## Module 7: Classical studies
- Rest stroke and free stroke (classical-rest-stroke, classical-free-stroke)
- An easy Carcassi study (classical-study-1)
- An easy Sor study (classical-study-2)

## Module 8: Consolidation
- Checklist: every chord, scale and technique from three grades (checkpoint-grade-3)
- Your repertoire: ten pieces from the library at tempo (checkpoint-grade-3, repertoire)
